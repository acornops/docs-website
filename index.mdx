AcornOps connects a central operations platform to Kubernetes clusters and Linux/systemd VMs through outbound target connectors. Teams use the management console to investigate live state, coordinate specialist automation, and review policy-gated remediation.

## What AcornOps provides

- **Workspace isolation** for members, roles, targets, automation, integrations, and history.
- **A shared target model** for Kubernetes clusters and Linux VMs.
- **Target-native investigations** using live inventory and bounded tools.
- **Specialist Agents and Workflows** for reusable, reviewable automation.
- **Layered authorization** across the user, workspace, target, run, and exact tool.
- **Self-hosted deployment** through Kubernetes or a single-VM Compose stack.
