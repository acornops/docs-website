title: "Get started on an existing platform"
description: "Join a workspace and complete a first safe investigation without deploying AcornOps"
---

Use this path when your organization already operates AcornOps. You do not need the Helm chart, Compose stack, Postgres, Redis, or platform secrets.

## Before you begin

Ask your workspace owner or administrator for:

- the management console URL,
- a workspace invitation or existing membership,
- the role you should have,
- a target you may inspect.

Your role controls whether you can connect targets, read logs, start runs, use write-capable tools, manage integrations, or approve changes.

## 1. Sign in

Open the management console URL supplied by your administrator. Sign in with one of the methods enabled for that deployment, such as its OIDC provider or username and password.

If you received a workspace invitation, accept it with the same identity that the invitation targets.

## 2. Open your workspace

Select the workspace your team uses. A workspace contains its members, targets, specialist Agents, Workflows, integrations, and operational history.

If you can sign in but cannot see the expected workspace, ask a workspace owner to verify your membership. Do not create a separate workspace as a workaround.

## 3. Find a connected target

Open a Kubernetes cluster or Linux VM that reports as connected. Review its inventory and current findings before starting a run.

If your role includes target management and no target exists, follow:

- [Connect a Kubernetes cluster](/use/connect-kubernetes)
- [Connect a Linux VM](/use/connect-vm)

## 4. Run a read-only investigation

Start with a read-only target investigation. Use a narrow operational question, review the tool scope, and observe the events as AcornOps gathers evidence.

Follow [Run your first investigation](/use/first-investigation) for the complete flow.
